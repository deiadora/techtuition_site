Human-Tested Methodology

The 4-Dimension Process Matrix

The exact delivery framework used to architect Design Operations at Coursera and AI Product Lifecycles at Airbnb. Applied across each agreed delivery phase (Discover, Define, Design, Build, Ship):

1. Cadences & Rituals Exact ceremonies attended by each function. Cuts meeting bloat and clarifies who is synchronous vs. async.
2. Active Responsibilities Explicit functional tasks owned during the phase. Eliminates role ambiguity and duplicate work.
3. Concrete Deliverables Unambiguous gate artifacts shipped before advancing (PRD, design spec, code branch, eval rubric).
4. Shared Resources & Repos Direct links to working boards, repositories, and documentation. Total cross-functional transparency.
